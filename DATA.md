# DATA.md

- **Data in this repo:** fully synthetic, generated by `src/data.py` (seed 42):
  skin-tone backgrounds with rotated, border-wobbled dark ellipses as lesions
  and exact paired masks. No real images, no patients, no personal data.
- **Not ISIC:** no ISIC Archive or ISIC Challenge data is used or redistributed.
- **Using real data later:** use an official ISIC release, follow its licence
  and split definitions, record the exact version in the README, and never
  reuse classification labels as segmentation masks.
